# Code of Conduct

LeanLocal welcomes constructive technical participation.

Be respectful, specific, and focused on the work. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable.

Disagreements about design should be handled with evidence, reproducible tests,
and clear technical reasoning.

Security-sensitive reports should use the private route described in
SECURITY.md rather than a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that breach these
rules or create unacceptable privacy, security, or maintenance risk.
